The core idea

Accounting is the structured way a business records what it owns, what it owes, and how it earns. The fundamental equation keeps every business record balanced:

Assets = Liabilities + Capital
Everything a business owns is funded either by borrowed money or the owner’s investment.

In Tally Prime, this equation comes alive through ledgers and vouchers. First, let’s simulate a simple company setup.
